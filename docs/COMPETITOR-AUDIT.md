# Competitor audit summary (November 2026)

Sites reviewed: dataocean.com, infor.com (ERP LN, industrial manufacturing, engineering & construction), acumatica.com, odoo.com, netsuite.com (ERP), epicor.com, scnsoft.com and itransition.com (oil & gas ERP). These are summarised in our own words.

## What they do well
- **Hero:** the H1 states an outcome or category and the hero offers two CTAs: a demo or consultation, plus a low-commitment option.
- **Self-segmenting entry points:**
  - Intent cards, e.g. "I want to leave spreadsheets" (Acumatica)
  - Choose by company size (Odoo)
  - Enterprise vs SMB switch (Infor)
- **Page structure:** modules grid → 4 benefits → 6–9 capability headings → industries → FAQ → short form.
- **Price signals:** NetSuite has a "How much does it cost?" section and Odoo shows pricing on the page.
- **Industry pages:** "[Industry] ERP" H1, challenges, capabilities, proof, resources, then a form (Infor, Itransition).
- **Trust stack:** years in business, project counts, certifications, partner badges and integration logos.

## Their weaknesses (our opportunities)
- **DataOcean SEO:**
  - Content is rendered client-side.
  - Stat counters read "0+" to crawlers.
  - The schema contradicts the brand.
  - There are no industry pages and no FAQ.
- **Infor FAQ schema:** Infor shows FAQs but has no FAQPage schema.
- **No software + marketing combination:** none of the audited vendors offers software delivery together with SEO and digital marketing. VQBYTE's position: **we build the system and bring the customers.**
- **Enterprise focus:** enterprise vendors hide cost and long timelines. We publish how pricing works and use phased go-live.

## Applied on our site
- Server-rendered pages with unique titles, meta descriptions and H1s, plus Service, FAQPage, BreadcrumbList, Organization and Article schema.
- "What do you need?" intent cards on the home page.
- One page per service and per industry, cross-linked.
- A "How pricing works" section and FAQs answering cost and timeline.
- No invented proof. Add real case studies, testimonials and client logos once approved, because named outcomes convert best.

## Keyword clusters (priorities inferred, volumes not measured)
- **ERP:** ERP software, custom ERP development, ERP for small business, ERP implementation.
- **CRM:** CRM software, custom CRM, WhatsApp CRM, sales CRM.
- **LMS:** LMS development, corporate LMS, custom learning management system.
- **Inventory:** inventory management software, warehouse management system, barcode inventory.
- **Engineering:** custom software development, web application development, mobile app development, API integration.
- **Growth:** SEO services, B2B SEO, digital marketing agency, Google Ads management.
- **Industries:** oil and gas ERP, manufacturing ERP, distribution ERP, construction ERP, 3PL software, field service software.
